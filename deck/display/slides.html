Diagnosing Display

We’re testing three potential contributors to Display performance.

Display performance is an outcome of an interconnected system. We need to isolate the variables before prescribing the solution.

01

Ad experience & media quality

Are we creating ad experiences shoppers actually see and engage with?

Investigate

Placement and inventory quality · Viewability · Ad relevance · Creative experience · Frequency · Where ads appear across Walmart · Differences in performance by placement or environment

02

Advertiser setup & workflow

Are we making it too difficult to build a campaign positioned to succeed?

Investigate

Audience selection · Targeting decisions · Budget configuration · Goals and objectives · Item selection · Creative setup · Workflow comprehension · Recommendations and guidance

03

Delivery, decisioning & optimization

Given a well-configured campaign and viable ad opportunity, is the system efficiently finding and serving the right impression?

Investigate

Eligible inventory · Auction and serving logic · Audience match and available reach · Budget pacing · Bid and optimization logic · Creative eligibility · Frequency constraints · Delivery opportunities · Optimization toward the selected objective

Our jobThe answer may be one. It may be all three. Our job is to isolate the variables rather than assume the cause.

Three buckets: what the shopper sees, what the advertiser builds, and what the system does with it. Only the middle one is primarily an experience problem, and we say so. The discipline is not picking a favorite before the evidence does.

Guidance, not noise

Recommendations only help when advertisers can trust them.

Guidance should reduce decision complexity. When relevance, context or expected value is unclear, it can create more complexity instead.

Historical assumption

More recommendations = more optimization opportunity.

What we’re learning

A recommendation without sufficient relevance, context or credibility can make the advertiser’s decision harder rather than easier.

The goalThe goal isn’t recommendation volume. It’s better advertiser decisions.

What we need

Three commitments make this work.

The strategy only holds if diagnosis, ownership and measurement are agreed before solutions scale.

Align

Diagnose Display across all three contributors before prescribing a solution, and use it as the first proof point for this approach.

Staff

Stand up a joint diagnosis with Display Product, Sales and Media Insights, with shared access to behavioral, setup and delivery data.

Measure

Agree the outcome measures for Goal-Based Buying, recommendations and shared capabilities before any of them scale.

These are proposals, not decisions already taken. Align is about the approach; Staff is about who does the diagnosis; Measure is about how we will know. If only one lands today, make it Align.
